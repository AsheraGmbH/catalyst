HOLLY

Ilex Opaca, known as American Holly, intertwines with rich traditions across cultures. In Celtic lore, it's a symbol of luck and eternal life, and Christianity sees it as a representation of Christ's sacrifice. Holly also features in British legends, symbolizing truth and justice, and gains literary fame in J.K. Rowling's 'Harry Potter' as the material for Harry's wand.

walnut

Juglans Regia, or Walnut, is steeped in enchantment and mythology. In Italy, it's linked with the legendary 'city of witches' Benevento, where an ancient walnut tree was a mythical meeting place for sorcery and inspired tales like Paganini's 'Le Streghe'. In Greek lore, it symbolizes prophetic power and eternal love, with Dionysius transforming his beloved into a walnut tree. This rich blend of mystery and romance makes the Walnut tree a captivating symbol of mystique and inspiration.

Aesculus, known as Buckeye, is a tree with a legacy of utility and natural beauty. In Native American tradition, its fruit was used in innovative fishing techniques, showcasing a harmony with nature. Buckeye's blend of tradition, healing, and aesthetic appeal makes it a symbol of resilience and versatility.
