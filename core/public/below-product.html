Titanium Grip

The grip is forged from 18 grams of pure titanium, guaranteeing an unparalleled writing experience. The feel of the titanium offers a sense of solidity in hand, and the mirror polish, unexpectedly, ensures a steady grip with minimal grip pressure required. The significant diameter of 12 mm is based on research into long-term writing comfort and ergonomics.

The Nib

Choose Gold, Steel or Platinum for a unique touch. Our nibs are completely smooth for a reason: to showcase the beauty of the metal. Just like our minimalist design aims to create a space for reflection and flow by showcasing pure wood and titanium, so too, can you enjoy the shimmer of polished gold, platinum or steel, without any distractions.

The Art of Lacquering

Incredibly difficult to make, we needed to construct new machines to be able to turn the shape of a perfect ellipsoid out of wood. Each pen is then lacquered by hand many times, each layer of lacquer is left to dry for days and hand polished before the next, to ensure that the beauty of the wood will be protected, and that the surface will shine for generations.

ALL TITANIUM ASSEMBLY

Just like the grip, so too are the rings that join the grip to the wood sections made from titanium. We are proud of not using any plastic threads in our pens. No matter how often you uncap and re-cap your pen, solid titanium on titanium threads will never fail or wear out.

Holly

Ilex Opaca, known as American Holly, intertwines with rich traditions across cultures. In Celtic lore, it's a symbol of luck and eternal life, and Christianity sees it as a representation of Christ's sacrifice. Holly also features in British legends, symbolizing truth and justice, and gains literary fame in J.K. Rowling's 'Harry Potter' as the material for Harry's wand.

Olive

Olea Europaea, an enduring symbol in the Mediterranean, represents peace, wisdom, and abundance, cherished by ancient Greeks and Romans. Athena's olive tree, famed for its fruitful bounty, symbolized prosperity and triumph in Greek lore. Romans revered it as a token of peace and glory, reflecting a rich cultural heritage. Today, its legacy as a symbol of peace and wisdom endures, inspiring a sense of historical connection and timeless elegance.

ELM BURL

Ulmus, the storied Elm, embodies resilience and revolution. In mythology, it's a symbol of hope and creation, from Greek dreams to Germanic origins. Historically, it represents the spirit of change, marking revolutions and strength in British longbow craftsmanship. The Elm's deep-rooted legacy in various cultures offers a connection to history, resilience, and transformation.

Walnut

Juglans Regia, or Walnut, is steeped in enchantment and mythology. In Italy, it's linked with the legendary 'city of witches' Benevento, where an ancient walnut tree was a mythical meeting place for sorcery and inspired tales like Paganini's 'Le Streghe'. In Greek lore, it symbolizes prophetic power and eternal love, with Dionysius transforming his beloved into a walnut tree. This rich blend of mystery and romance makes the Walnut tree a captivating symbol of mystique and inspiration.

Aesculus, known as Buckeye, is a tree with a legacy of utility and natural beauty. In Native American tradition, its fruit was used in innovative fishing techniques, showcasing a harmony with nature.
